# Privacy Policy for Thinam-oru-kural

**Last updated: October 26, 2023**

Thinam-oru-kural ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**Thinam-oru-kural is an offline-first application.**
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or location).
* **Local Storage:** The App uses a local database and preferences to store your kural progress and notification settings. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

## 2. Permissions
The App requires the following permissions to function:
* **Notifications:** To deliver the daily Thirukkural.
* **Boot Completed:** To ensure your daily notification schedule is maintained after a device restart.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
